The [scripted traces](/posts/scripted-traces/) show Qwen reading before it answers and writing valid JSON. Now we train on them with [LoRA](/posts/lora-from-scratch/) and rerun the [Day 7 eval](/posts/the-day7-eval/) with nothing changed. It uses the same {day7.tasks.length} tasks, the same three sampling seeds and the same limits.

The exit check, written before the run, asks whether the gain beats the spread between training seeds. The mean of the two adapters is {f3(mean)}. Minus the base {f3(base.pass_1_mean)}, that's a gain of {f3(gain)}, against a gap of {f3(gap)} between the adapters. The check passes by a factor of {Math.round(gain / gap)}.

## Both Day 7 failures are gone

Day 7 found two problems. The model answered without calling a tool, and its `write_file` JSON broke. The valid-call rate rose from {f3(base.valid_call_rate)} to above {f3(Math.min(s0.valid_call_rate, s1.valid_call_rate))}. pass^1 counting only runs with a valid call is {f3(s0.pass_1_with_valid_call)} and {f3(s1.pass_1_with_valid_call)}, equal to plain pass^1, so every pass came from a run that used a tool. The lucky guesses of the base model are gone.

## Debugging is still out of reach

`tests` tasks plant a bug and ask the model to fix it. The adapters passed {testsPassed} of {testsRuns} such runs. Seed 1 hit the turn limit in {s1.stops.turns} runs, including all {testsTried(eval1).filter((r) => r.stop === "turns").length} of its `tests` runs. It loops on reading files and running tests instead of writing a fix. The traces showed the right sequence, run the tests, read, fix, run again, but {traces.by_kind.tests} examples of a bug fix aren't enough to teach what the fix should be.

## The training seed matters more than the sampling seed

Within one adapter, the three sampling seeds span {f3(range(s0))} for seed 0 and {f3(range(s1))} for seed 1. Between the adapters, pass^1 moves by {f3(gap)}. Two adapters trained on the same data with the same settings behave differently, and the per-kind rows show it: seed 1 is better at `answer` and `hidden`, and seed 0 is better at `file`. One training run would have hidden that.

Decision: keep. The fine-tune passes the exit check, and both Day 7 failure modes are gone.

<Label>Skipped</Label>

## Not built

- **A held-out training loss.** All {traces.tasks} traces went into training. The eval on a different repository is the held-out test, and it's the number that matters.
- **More training seeds.** Two seeds meet the exit check. With a seed gap of {f3(gap)}, a finer comparison between settings would need more.
- **Tuning.** Rank, learning rate and epochs stayed at the frozen values. Changing them after seeing the eval would turn the eval into a training signal.
